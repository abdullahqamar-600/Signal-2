Travel & Hotel · Omaha, NE

Getting
to Omaha

Travel and lodging are owner-funded. We've gathered the airport and hotel recommendations so planning takes ten minutes, not an afternoon.

  1. OMA Airport · Eppley

    Fly into Eppley Airfield

    20–25 minutes from Signal Home Office. Serviced by every major US carrier with non-stops from most large hubs.

    • Direct flights from most major US hubs
    • Rideshare and rental cars at the curb
    • Short-term and long-term parking on site
    • Plan an early-afternoon arrival on Monday, October 5 if joining the Kids Against Hunger kickoff
  2. EMB Hotel · Recommended

    Embassy Suites La Vista

    By Hilton, attached to the La Vista Conference Center. Ten minutes from Signal Home Office and walkable to dining.

    • Complimentary breakfast and parking
    • Group rate on hold for the summit dates
    • Walkable to restaurants and dining
    • Attendees are welcome to stay at any hotel of their choosing
  3. HQ Destination · Signal

    Signal Home Office

    Three days, one room. The summit runs from Signal's home office in Omaha — close to the hotel and the airport, easy to find.

    • 3880 S. 149th St., Ste. 106, Omaha, NE 68144
    • 10 minutes from Embassy Suites La Vista
    • 20–25 minutes from Eppley Airfield
    • Parking on site for attendees

Planning to come early?

Monday October 5 has two optional kickoff events. Aim for an early-afternoon arrival so you can join Kids Against Hunger and the evening at Vala's Pumpkin Patch with the rest of the group.

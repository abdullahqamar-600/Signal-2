Getting
to Omaha

Travel and lodging are owner-funded. We've gathered the airport and hotel recommendations so planning takes ten minutes, not an afternoon.

Destination

Signal Home Office, 3880 S. 149th St., Ste. 106, Omaha, NE 68144

Eppley AirfieldOMA

20–25 minutes from Signal Home Office. Serviced by all major airlines.

  • Rideshare and rental cars at the curb
  • Short-term and long-term parking on site
  • Direct flights from most major US hubs

Plan to arrive by early afternoon on Monday, October 5 if you'd like to join the Kids Against Hunger kickoff.

Recommended hotel

Embassy Suites La Vista

10 minutes from Signal Home Office. By Hilton, in the La Vista Conference Center.

  • Complimentary breakfast
  • Complimentary parking
  • Walkable to restaurants and dining

Attendees are welcome to stay at any hotel of their choosing.

Planning to come early?

Monday October 5 has two optional kickoff events. Aim for an early-afternoon arrival so you can join Kids Against Hunger and the evening at Vala's Pumpkin Patch with the rest of the group.

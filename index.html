About the summit

A New Format for Owners.

Leadership Summit combines the former Owner Summit, Financial Summit & Vision Summit into one event. Owners will evaluate their business performance, identify opportunities with the greatest impact, and develop a strategic plan that drives priorities throughout the year.

What to expect

Three days focused on
franchise growth.

  1. Oct 5
    Optional Kickoff Events

    Start the week early.

    Two optional events planned ahead of Leadership Summit.

    • Afternoon — Kids Against Hunger Volunteer Event. Work alongside other Signal franchisees to pack meals for those in need through a friendly team-based competition.
    • Evening — Vala’s Pumpkin Patch. Join the group for food and an evening at one of Omaha’s favorite fall destinations.
  2. Oct 6
    Tuesday

    Executive Vision & Business Updates.

    Hear directly from Reed and the Executive Team about:

    • Upcoming Signal initiatives and strategic direction
    • Operational priorities and business updates
    • What to expect in the months ahead
  3. Oct 7–8
    Wednesday & Thursday

    Strategic Planning for the Upcoming Year.

    Owners will develop a strategic plan for the year ahead and map out their first 90 days. They’ll also have opportunities to meet one-on-one with Home Office leaders to discuss growth opportunities in their local market.

Why attend

Why attend
Leadership Summit?

Over three days, you’ll hear what other franchises are doing successfully, take part in breakout sessions, and spend time with others who are navigating the same opportunities and priorities.

You’ll leave with

  1. 01 Sales and operational strategies being used in other markets.
  2. 02 Examples of how franchisees are approaching leadership and growth.
  3. 03 A strategic plan and roadmap for the first 90 days of the new year.

## Visual notes for future sessions

- **Palette match:** The room interiors are saturated Signal Blue with orange
  logo accents — they slot naturally into the 70/20/10 palette without color
  correction.
- **Light:** The outdoor candids are shot at golden hour (warm, soft). Indoor
  sessions are lit by overhead LED — slightly cooler, more clinical. Mixing
  the two requires care; avoid placing a warm outdoor shot adjacent to a cool
  indoor shot without a buffer.
- **Faces:** Many shots include identifiable attendees with name tags. For
  early/internal mockups this is fine; for any external use confirm release
  permissions before publishing.
- **Pumpkins on stage** in a few shots (e.g. `-1144`, `-1681`) tie naturally
  to the 2026 event's October dates — lean into these where October mood is
  wanted.
- **Aspect ratios:** Most are 3:2 (landscape) or 2:3 (portrait); the
  `OwnersSummit_10.2024_-36.jpg` panorama is much wider (~3:1) — best used
  as a true banner.

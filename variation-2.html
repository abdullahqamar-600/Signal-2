Frequently
asked questions.

01 Who should attend? +

Leadership Summit is designed primarily for Signal franchise owners, executive directors, directors, and other key employees within franchise organizations.

02 Can I bring members of my leadership team? +

Yes. Leadership Summit was intentionally expanded to allow franchise leadership teams to attend together.

03 What types of sessions will be offered? +

Sessions include executive team updates, leadership development, sales and operational breakout trainings, franchise owner-led best practices, and interactive discussions built around the day-to-day realities of running a Signal franchise.

04 What is included with registration? +

Registration includes access to Summit sessions, breakout trainings, networking opportunities, and scheduled activities. Hotel accommodations are not included.

05 Do I need to attend all three days? +

We strongly recommend participating in the full Summit to take part in the complete schedule of events.

06 Is the October 5 kickoff required? +

No, but arriving by early afternoon on October 5 allows you to participate in the Kids Against Hunger volunteer event and the evening at Vala’s Pumpkin Patch.

07 Where will Leadership Summit be held? +

Leadership Summit will take place at Signal Home Office in Omaha, Nebraska — 3880 S. 149th St., Ste. 106, Omaha, NE 68144.

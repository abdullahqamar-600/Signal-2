what this event is

A new format for owners and leadership teams.

Leadership Summit combines the former Owner Summit and Vision Summit into one event for franchise owners, executive directors, directors, and other key employees across the Signal network.

The Summit includes sessions and discussions that have never been offered before — covering the topics franchise leaders are asking for most. Throughout the week you'll hear business updates, learn best practices for sales and operations, and hear how other markets are managing and supporting their teams.

Three days, session by session.

faqs

Before you book the flight.

Who should attend?

Leadership Summit is designed primarily for Signal franchise owners, executive directors, directors, and other key employees within franchise organizations.

Can I bring members of my leadership team?

Yes. Leadership Summit was intentionally expanded to allow franchise leadership teams to attend together.

What types of sessions will be offered?

Sessions will include executive team updates, leadership development, sales and operational breakout trainings, franchise owner-led best practices, and interactive discussions built around the day-to-day realities of running a Signal franchise.

What is included with registration?

Registration includes access to Summit sessions, breakout trainings, networking opportunities, and scheduled activities. Hotel accommodations are not included.

Do I need to attend all three days?

We strongly recommend participating in the full Summit to take part in the complete schedule of events.

Is the October 5 kickoff required?

No, but arriving early on October 5 allows you to participate in the Kids Against Hunger event and the evening at Vala's Pumpkin Patch.

Where will Leadership Summit be held?

Leadership Summit will take place at Signal Home Office in Omaha, Nebraska.
